# Privacy Policy — Check.

**Last Updated:** May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data of users of the **Check.** mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") is collected, processed, and protected. The Application is provided by **SzpontCompany**, located in Zielona Góra, Poland.

---

### 1. What data do we collect?

When you use the Application, we may collect and process the following information:

**1.1. Data provided by the User:**
- Registration data: email address, username, password (in encrypted form).
- Profile data: avatar, language preferences (PL/ENG), theme settings (light/dark).

**1.2. Activity and habit data:**
- Information about your created, saved, and completed habits.
- Data from your device's sensors (e.g., pedometer) to count steps and award achievements.

**1.3. Location data:**
- With your explicit consent, we collect location data (GPS) for the Maps module, accurate step/distance tracking, and sharing your location with selected friends.

**1.4. Technical and automated data:**
- Device identifiers, IP address, operating system type (Android).
- **Captcha** verification results to prevent abuse and bot activity.
- Analytics data and advertising IDs (for displaying ads).

### 2. How do we use your data?

Your data is used to:

2.1. Provide Application services (e.g., cloud saving of habits, cross-device synchronization).

2.2. Operate social features, including the **"Habit Battle"** group competition system and real-time location sharing with friends on the map.

2.3. Update statistics (e.g., for the Home Screen Widget) and process step counts.

2.4. Send Push notifications regarding challenges, events, and habit reminders.

2.5. Display personalized or non-personalized advertisements within the App (depending on your consent).

2.6. Ensure account security and prevent fraud (e.g., through Captcha).

### 3. Who do we share your data with?

3.1. **Other Users:** Within the **Habit Battle** feature, members of your group can see your username, avatar, step count, and habit progress. Your location on the Map is visible only to friends you explicitly choose to share it with.

3.2. **Google LLC (Firebase):** The Application uses Google Firebase infrastructure for authentication, secure cloud data storage (database), and crash/analytics tracking.

3.3. **Advertising Partners:** We may share anonymized advertising identifiers with ad network providers to monetize the Application.

### 4. User Rights

You have full control over your data. You have the right to:

4.1. Access and update your personal information.

4.2. Delete your account and permanently erase all associated data (option available within the App's settings).

4.3. Revoke your consent for location tracking or push notifications (via your Android system settings).

4.4. Manage your advertising preferences.

### 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures (including encryption and token-based authentication) to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Our primary database is hosted in the secure Google Firebase environment.
